How Trackfound Works

A transparent rule-based matching system that protects privacy while recovering lost property.

1

Publish Report

Post details about what you lost or found. Trackers keep private unique clues to verify real ownership.

2

Smart Match Score

Our automated rule-based algorithm compares title, category, location, date, and color to generate match probability (0–100%).

3

Safe Verification & Chat

Claim items by answering private verification prompts. Chat securely inside Trackfound without revealing phone or email.

Public Safety & Protection Guidelines

  • Meet in Public Places: Always meet at police stations, mall help desks, or railway inquiry counters.
  • Never Pay Upfront Fees: Legitimate Trackers will never request courier money or reward payments upfront.
  • Redact Sensitive Documents: Never post unmasked photos of Aadhaar, Passport, or Credit Cards.
  • Verify Ownership Clues: Ask specific questions about item contents or serial numbers.
